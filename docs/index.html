Palisades lift-hold rulebook

Palisades Tahoe and Alpine Meadows lifts hold, open late or close by rules nobody published. We recovered them from eight winters of the ops account's posts and the weather station record, kept what was observed apart from what was inferred, and tested every rule on a winter it never saw.

Day-level wind rule, held-out winters (mean F1)0.82vs 0.57 for the naive “gust ≥ 25” rule
Per-lift two-stage rule, held-out winters (mean F1)0.25vs 0.26 naive: a tie, wins 3 of 8 winters. Reported as a negative result.
Evidence base8 winters1,071 days with lift posts, 36 lifts with rules

What we tried that did not work

Wind is the rule. The other cause families are not recoverable from these weather files, and we say so.

lightning / ice / rain52avy ≥ 4.00.33, 0.22, 0.21, 0.09, 0.00, 0.00
  • Snow safety and avalanche control: a two-feature rule (new snow and snowpack depth) beat “always say hold” in only 3 of 8 held-out winters. Patrol decisions are not in the weather data.
  • Mechanical holds: no weather feature predicts them, as expected. They stay in an “unexplained” bucket, labelled as such.
  • Lightning, ice, rain: too few days (about 50) for a stable rule.
  • Per-lift prediction: averages the same F1 as the naive rule and is far below the day-level wind rule. Knowing a windy day is easy; knowing which chair stops is not, from these features. The per-lift thresholds in the table below are descriptive (hold rate above vs below), not a validated predictor.

Limits we know about

  • Gust units come straight from the station feed and were not independently confirmed here; read thresholds as relative, not as a posted limit.
  • The day-level rule uses the whole day's maximum gust. A morning-only version was not fitted on held-out winters, so the replay is a same-day check, not a forecast.
  • Labels come from a parser (89 golden test cases) plus two independent model readers; 1 to 5% of posts per winter stay unmatched.
  • Whether pre-event data pulls are acceptable to the organizers is unconfirmed. All code in this repo was written at the event.
