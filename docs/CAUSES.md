# Inferred causes for holds whose post names none

Generated by `python3 -I scripts/infer_causes.py`. **Inferred, not observed**: the observed `cause` fields are never changed. Output: `data/derived/cause_inferred.csv`.

## What it does

- 5,584 in-season hold or closure lift-days. 4,036 have a post that names exactly one cause family (wind, patrol or avalanche control, mechanical, weather); 1,376 name none.
- For each cause family a logistic regression is fitted on the named holds, using: crest gust, hours at 60 mph or more, new snow, snow water, avalanche danger, base temperature, spring, hour of the first post, cause words in the day's OTHER posts (avalanche control, power, lightning, ice, visibility, mechanical, flooding or road) and whether the whole mountain closed.
- Accuracy is measured leave-one-winter-out on the named holds: each winter is scored by a model that never saw it.

## How well it works

- Right on 80% of named holds, against 67% for always guessing the most common family. Called with probability 0.6 or more: 87% right, on 82% of holds.

| Named cause | Holds | Recovered by the model |
|---|---|---|
| wind | 2720 | 93% |
| patrol | 462 | 49% |
| mechanical | 382 | 57% |
| weather | 472 | 49% |

## What the unstated holds look like

| Inferred cause | Unstated holds | Of those, confident (0.6+) |
|---|---|---|
| wind | 873 (63%) | 596 |
| patrol | 260 (19%) | 70 |
| mechanical | 141 (10%) | 61 |
| weather | 102 (7%) | 47 |

774 of 1,376 unstated holds (56%) get a confident inferred cause; the rest stay unexplained.

## Limits

- The model learns what named holds look like, so it assumes unstated holds come from the same mix of causes. If operators skip naming a cause for a particular reason, that assumption fails.
- Weather families overlap: a hold named "weather" and one named "wind" can sit on the same day.
- Patrol includes avalanche control and snow safety. Whole-mountain closures with no cause word are mostly inferred as wind or weather by construction.
- Use the inferred cause for exploration and for filling gaps in charts. Do not quote it as what the operators said.
