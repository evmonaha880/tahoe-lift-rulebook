# Decisions

All dated 2026-11-07 unless noted.

| # | Decision | Rejected / why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Build in the **Software Archaeology** lane | Other lanes weighed. Games (done a lot), business systems (not interesting to the owner), Kodak edge codes (a lookup table, image-analyzer ban risk), traffic lights (no data), Soviet maps (no free scan found), stock data (crowded, off-interest). Apollo Guidance Computer and Bach chorale rules were finalists. Tahoe won on personal use and data in hand. |
| 2 | **Palisades Tahoe + Alpine Meadows is the core** | Heavenly has fewer winters of usable weather history (its stations ended mid-2025) and the ops feed is less detailed. Palisades has about 10 winters of both posts and station data. |
| 3 | **Not a wind-only hypothesis** | Wind is the biggest stated cause but many holds give no cause. Rules must cover snow loading, temperature and rain-vs-snow, pressure, and leave an honest "unexplained" bucket. |
| 4 | Heavenly, Northstar, Kirkwood, Mt Rose **later** | Out of scope today. Structure the repo with one folder per mountain so they can be added. Northstar, Kirkwood and Mt Rose looked thin on station data. |
| 5 | "What to do this morning" module **later** | Not part of the hackathon. It will read the same rulebook plus live feeds. |
| 6 | **Synoptic API is dead** for history | The account's token is refused for all history calls. Replaced by the Western Weather Group public report builder. |
| 7 | Weather: **hourly plus 15-minute** from the Western Weather Group builder | 1-minute returned nothing. Pull all fields at hourly; 15-minute for finer wind timing. Redundant derived fields (wind chill, heat index, daily max/min, equipment health) are dropped at analysis time, not at pull time. |
| 8 | Snow: **SNOTEL 784** hourly for 18 winters | Free, verified, includes wind since 2008; a long-history backup for station wind. |
| 9 | X posts **one winter at a time**, with a verifier and a rules ledger | A bulk pull would hide format drift. See `PARSING_RULES.md`. |
| 10 | X query = **originals only** for now; **replies decision pending** | Retweets add cost without information. Replies may carry cause information; size and sample them before deciding. |
| 11 | Winter order: 2023-24, 2024-25, 2025-26, then older | 2023-24 has an earlier independent table for checking and is a strong El Niño winter by ONI (+1.84) but moderate by RONI (+1.13); see DATA_SOURCES.md. The next two show drift up to the present. |
| 12 | Test with **leave-one-winter-out** across winters | One winter plus a few days is too weak to trust. |
| 13 | Label every winter by ONI/RONI; El Niño via **archetype days and a 2023-24 replay** | A synthetic whole-winter simulator is not supported by the data available. |
| 14 | Parsing rules are recovered with **observed vs inferred** tags, like the lift rules | Keeps the story consistent and the claims honest. |
| 15 | Tools: **Subquadratic** and **Meta Muse API** as independent blind labelers of the posts (they never see the parser's output) | Used for verification only. Template discovery was done by reading posts directly; no whole-winter Subquadratic read was run. Gemini and MongoDB not used. |
| 16 | Docs split: **public repo docs + a private handoff file outside the repo** | Public docs are safe for judges to read. The private file holds spend, credential locations and session state. |
| 17 | Raw pull files not in git; the app shows the text of public posts beside their parsed events | The raw X files are bulky and re-pullable, so they stay out of git. Evan decided on 2026-11-07 to show post text in the app because it is public and central to the project. |
| 18 | **Prize tracks: only Subquadratic and Meta Muse are used, as blind labelers inside the pipeline, and measured.** Fetch.ai, Gemini and MongoDB are not used and not claimed. | Fetch.ai's track needs an Agentverse agent speaking the Agent Chat Protocol; it was reviewed and not pursued. Cash prizes are upside, not a plan. |
| 19 | **UI: a static replay app in `docs/app/`**, built separately from the data work. No ASI:One agent. | The rulebook is stored as data, so any front end can read it. |
| 20 | **Avalanche danger ratings** from the public Sierra Avalanche Center API are a candidate feature for patrol-related holds | Free, no key, daily since about 2020. Zone coverage of Palisades unverified. See `DATA_SOURCES.md` section 5. |
| 21 | Alpine's "Meadow" and Palisades' "Mountain Meadow" are **two lifts**; "upper mountain" follows the operators' own lists | Read from the posts and a public lifts table, not from AI answers where they disagreed. See `rules/lift_groups.json`. |
| 22 | Squaw One = Wa She Shu, Squaw Creek = Resort Chair; old names folded into the new | Timing alone suggested the opposite pairing; a public source settled it. Tucker Carpet to Kaya Carpet is unverified and not merged. |
| 23 | Hedged wording ("expect delays", "may go on hold") is a **warning**, not a hold or delay | Predictions are not events. The blind readers have no warning class, so they disagree on these by design. |
| 24 | Held-out posts are for **measuring only**; an error seen there is fixed only if the same pattern appears in training posts | Keeps the held-out numbers honest. |
| 25 | Funitel-top wind is **estimated** where the station was off (2.16 + 0.13 x Siberia + 0.72 x Belmont, R2 0.89); 2025-26 uses the estimate throughout | The source returns no rows for those dates; the 2025-26 sensor reads differently from earlier years. Flagged in the data. |
| 26 | Lift-level results use **scheduled lift-days only** | Lift-days that exist only because a post named the lift as held are 76% held by construction and inflate every method. Both are reported in `RESULTS.md`. |
| 27 | **The app is a view of the pipeline, not a second analysis.** Every score, rule, odds table, lift grouping and reader-agreement figure in `docs/app/` is written by `scripts/build_app_model.py` from the repo's own outputs and checked by `scripts/check_app_model.py`; the page does no fitting or scoring of its own | The first version re-fitted its lines and re-scored its lift-level cards in the browser on a different row set, so its numbers contradicted `RESULTS.md`. The frozen first version is the git tag `v1-freeze`. |
| 28 | **Whole-mountain closures are events.** Rule P-022 reads "Palisades Tahoe is CLOSED today", "ALL LIFTS CLOSED TODAY" and "will not have any lift operations" with no lift named; build_dataset.py expands them to every lift that normally runs on that side (`closed_all`, `expected`) | Before this, a day with only an "all lifts closed" post could drop out of the table, and the app showed it as unexplained. Evan decided on 2026-11-07 that these events matter. Patterns came from train posts only; held-out posts were not used to design the rule. |
| 29 | **Season windows** (`rules/season_windows.json`, effective windows in `data/derived/season_windows_effective.json`): open = earlier of the web date and the first roster day, close = later of the web date and the last day a post shows that side running; rows outside are flagged `in_season=0` and excluded from fitting | Reduces noise from pre-season storm posts and post-season days. Web dates are search-result evidence marked confirmed or unconfirmed in the file; the union keeps every real operating day. |
| 30 | **Multi-label cause tags** (`cause_tags`) beside the single `cause` | One post often names several causes ("high winds, avalanche control and grooming"); the single field keeps only the first family. Tags are post-level, not sentence-level. |
| 31 | **Gateway = the Tram and the Funitel.** The Base to Base Gondola has its own tier in the app | Matches the upper-mountain cascade analysis and the plain meaning (the two big lifts up to the upper mountain). |
| 32 | **Lift-level results use expected lift-days** (scheduled, or a normally running lift under a whole-side closure), replacing decision 26's scheduled-only set | Whole-mountain closure days have no roster post, so the lifts were never "scheduled" even though they were meant to run. Pooled lift-level F1 moved from 0.46 to 0.57 for per-lift rules at 50% odds. Day-level results are unchanged (8 of 8 winters). |
| 33 | **Storm days and wind duration are reported from counts, not fitted lines**: storm = 2+ in new snow, 1+ in snow water or avalanche danger 3+; duration = hours at 60 mph or more at Siberia, 6am to 4pm | These were found in the evening data review. Snow effect on wind-hold timing was NOT detectable once wind duration is controlled; snow and avalanche danger show up as late openings and closures. |
| 34 | **Closure kinds and the two-sided schedule.** Each whole-mountain closure is tagged storm, lightning, infrastructure, schedule (no cause, spring, before 10am) or unstated (`closure_kind`); the Base to Base Gondola has its own side (`link`) and counts as in season when either side is, and counts as closed only when both sides are | Palisades and Alpine do not always open and close together (Alpine often closes earlier in spring) and the gondola keeps its own timeline. Schedule closures are operations, not conditions. See `docs/CLOSURES.md`. |
| 35 | **Hedged wording is a post-level warning** (rule P-012h): "winds may impact lift operations", "please EXPECT DELAYS", "possible wind holds" in any post that has no warning yet; road and traffic delays are excluded | Decision 23 already says predictions are not events; 52 train posts had such wording and no warning. Always post-level, never attached to listed lifts. |
| 36 | **No reopening post the same day means the lift did not reopen** (`reopen_assumed_none`), stored beside the observed `reopen_posted` | Evan's assumption on 2026-11-07. It is weakest on calm days, where about four in ten holds have a posted reopening. |
| 37 | **Inferred causes are a separate file** (`data/derived/cause_inferred.csv`, `docs/CAUSES.md`) and never overwrite the observed `cause` | A hold with no named cause gets a likely family from weather and the day's other posts, scored leave-one-winter-out. Observed and inferred are never merged (the project's first rule). |
| 38 | **Winter 2020-21 stays in the replay and the day-level wind line but is out of lift-level fits** (`covid_era`, `fit_ok`); 2019-20 ends 2020-03-14 | Operating rules (reservations, capacity) made lifts run or hold for non-weather reasons: 30% of calm-day lift-days held against 9% elsewhere. Excluding it moved pooled lift-level F1 from 0.570 to 0.576. |
| 39 | **Day-level cause words** from every post that day (avalanche control, power, lightning, ice, visibility, mechanical, flood or road) are columns (`day_*`), and each closure carries a confidence from the two blind readers plus weather plausibility | Operators name avalanche control and power cuts in posts that do not produce a lift event; counting them recovers causes the event parser keeps as unstated. |
| 40 | **Closure scope, later-day, exception, hike-only and run fixes** (parser rules P-021 ALL_ON_HOLD with `except`, P-022 per sentence, hedge exclusions): "Palisades Tahoe Alpine" is the Alpine side, "all lifts at Palisades Tahoe" is the Palisades side, "closed for Tuesday 3/14" is a warning, "all lifts with the exception of X" carries an `except` list that build_dataset honours, hike-only access is a `partial`, and runs ("Mountain Run") are not lifts | Written the evening of 2026-11-07 after reading the held-out hand-check errors (under decision 24 this is allowed only where the pattern is also in train posts; scope exceptions are, the others are not). The closure rows of the hand check are therefore no longer blind; the 120 fresh random posts are. |
| 41 | **NWS alerts for the Greater Lake Tahoe Area zone** (`nws_*` columns, `scripts/pull_nws.py`, free) | Winter Storm and Blizzard alerts add a little for closures; wind alerts add nothing beyond measured gusts. `nws_pre_*` records what was in force at 6am. |
| 42 | **Wind at the hour of the call and a nearest-station gust per lift** (`gust_at_event`, `gust_prior3h`, `local_gust`) are columns, not rule inputs | The hour features confirm the ladder (Tram about 77 mph, Funitel 98, lower lifts 107 in the three hours before the call). The nearest-station mapping is unverified and helps only some lifts. |
| 43 | **Multi-model review is the check on parser changes**: three Claude models plus Meta Muse and Subquadratic grade the same sheet independently and the majority vote is the figure quoted; the two hackathon models need the project's lift aliases and cause conventions in the prompt or they over-flag | One model reader is a weak check; three agreeing readers (kappa 0.78) are stronger, but they are still models, so a human spot-check of the closure posts remains open. |
