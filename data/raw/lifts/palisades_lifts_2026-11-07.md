## Answers to the session's two questions
1. **"Upper mountain":** settled. See the last section of this file, "How the operators themselves use upper mountain". That section overrules anything above it.
2. **"Meadow" vs "Mountain Meadow":** they are two different lifts. Meadow is a 366 m beginner chair on the Alpine side (Scott/Lakeview area, next to Subway). Mountain Meadow is an 850 m chair at High Camp on the Palisades side. The session can split them by area: a post that names Alpine, Subway or Big Carpet means Meadow.

## Source notes
- The resort page and skiresort.com use slightly different names (Funitel vs Gold Coast Funitel, Red Dog vs Red Dog Express). Ahsoka and High Camp Carpet appear only on the resort page.

## How the operators themselves use "upper mountain" (read from this project's own posts, 2018-19 to 2025-26)
Method: searched all 7,044 train and held-out posts for "upper/lower mountain"; 304 mention it. Most of those are weather reports ("7 inches on our Upper Mountains") or parking ("Upper Mountain lots at Alpine are full"). Those are NOT lift groups. The lift-status posts below are. Two AI tools (Gemini, Google AI Mode) were also asked; where they disagree with the posts, the posts win.

**Palisades side, upper mountain (operators' own lists):** Aerial Tram, Funitel, Wa She Shu, Siberia, Headwall, Emigrant, Granite Chief, Gold Coast, Shirley Lake, Big Blue, Solitude, Silverado, Mountain Meadow, Bailey's Beach, Belmont, High Camp Carpet, Base to Base Gondola (held with the upper lifts in 2024-25).
- 2024-25: "Upper Mountain is off the schedule, including Wa She Shu, Tram, Funitel. Lower Mountain is on patrol hold (Exhibition, Far East, KT-22, Red Dog, Resort Chair)."
- 2022-23: "All upper mountain lifts removed from the schedule: Tram, Bailey's Beach, Gold Coast, Mountain Meadow, Shirley Lake, Siberia, Solitude."
- 2018-19: winds "across our upper mountains": Aerial Tram, Headwall, Siberia, Emigrant, Granite Chief, Solitude, Bailey's Beach, Mountain Meadow, Belmont.
**Palisades side, lower mountain:** KT-22, Exhibition, Far East, Red Dog, Resort Chair (First Venture and the carpets open with them).
**Disagreement with both AI tools:** both called Wa She Shu "lower" and the Tram and Funitel "connectors". The operators count all three as upper mountain. The Funitel is sometimes run on its own while the rest of the upper mountain is held ("Funitel for Mountain Run only"): treat that as an exception, not a different group.
**Olympic Lady and Broken Arrow (checked against 101 and 109 posts):** Olympic Lady = LOWER. The operators list it as open beside KT-22, Exhibition, Red Dog and Resort Chair while "All Upper Mountain Lifts" are on wind hold (2022-23, 2025-26). It does go on wind hold in very strong wind, but is named apart from the upper group. Broken Arrow = UPPER, moderate confidence. In 2018-19 it is held in the same wind-hold list as Bailey's Beach, Mountain Meadow, Belmont, Solitude, Granite Chief, Emigrant, Siberia and Headwall, and a 2022-23 status post lists it on patrol hold next to "All other upper mountain lifts". An AI answer called it "lower-to-mid"; the posts do not support that. Gold Coast Express vs Gold Coast Funitel naming is still unchecked.
**Alpine side:** operators rarely say "upper mountain" at Alpine. They name lifts: Summit, Scott, Alpine Bowl Chair, "all aerial lifts at Alpine". Yellow and Kangaroo are named separately ("also placed on Wind Hold"), so they are not part of "upper". Both AI tools agree on this split and the posts do not contradict it, but the Alpine evidence is thin.
